Hamburger

Description

An American classic, the flavour of meat, cheese and bread in one place, simple, juicy and tasty at the same time.

Is something that can't be missed in any BBQ and it is required to be there.

Ingredients

  • Ground Meat
  • Ketchup
  • Mustard
  • Mayonnaise
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Eggs
  • Hamburger Buns
  • Optionals:
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Pickles

Steps

  1. Put your ground meat inside of a big bowl and add Ketchup, Mustard, Salt, Pepper and a few Eggs.
  2. Mix and steer that ground meat inside the big bowl until all ingredients are one big blop of seasoned ground meat.
  3. Grab and make small balls of meat that you are gonna press in the grill (or Pan with a bit of oil) and then you are gonna let it cook.
  4. Flip the meat when one side is cooked, and repeat until both sides are cooked.
  5. Meanwhile the meat is being cooked, try adding Mayonnaise to your Hamburger buns.
  6. Add the cooked meat in the bun and you'll be done, you have a Hamburger, if you wanted cheese you could had put the cheese on top of the meat while cooking... or add it here!
  7. EXTRA: Add any of the Optional ingredients in between the bread and the meat to add extra flavours.
