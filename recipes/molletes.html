Molletes

Description

It's a Mexican food that is not only easy to make but also it's tasteful and can have multiple added flavour for anyone looking for a unique taste for this dish.

It can be done at MOST in 20 minutes and is easy to make multiple samples in one go.

Ingredients

  • Ground Beans (In any color)
  • Cheese
  • Roll of Bread
  • EXTRA: Butter
  • Any other eatable product you wanna add as a seasoning on top of the finished product

Steps

  1. Make a horizontal cut to the roll of bread so you have two halfs of the roll for each bread, one roll of bread can do two molletes.
  2. Spread evenly the beans on top of each half of bread; in case you wanna add extra flavour, add the butter first before the beans.
  3. Add the cheese on top of the beans and in the same way spread it evenly across the half bread.
  4. You'll put in the oven a temp of 250°F (120°C) and then add a timer of 10 to 15 minutes in the even, and remember to let it prepare before.
  5. Put your raw molletes in the oven and let them cook, remember to check them constantly around 7 to 8 minutes.
  6. When the cheese is starting to get golden it's a good time to stop the oven or if the timer runs out; and with that you are DONE.
  7. EXTRA: You can add anything on top of your molletes if you want something different.
